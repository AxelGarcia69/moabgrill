About Us

Our Story

The Sweet Savor of Gratitude and Grandma's Recipes: The New Moab Grill.

The Garcia family cooks! And now they present first-class cooking at the Moab Grill. As they enter their new family enterprise as both management and cook staff of this landmark restaurant, they are filled with gratitude to those who have helped them.

"Taste the spirit of Moab in every bite."

House favorites

What We're Known For

  1. 01

    Our Signature
    Burgers

    Big, bold and made for Moab people. From juicy beef classics to our unique bison creations, with more than 10 signature burgers to choose from… or build your own burger and create it exactly the way you like it!

  2. 02

    Legendary
    Grill

    Exceptional flavors and bold grill favorites define our signature selection, featuring premium ribeyes and sirloins, complemented by grilled shrimp, trout, fish & chips, and smoky BBQ ribs. Served with your choice of two sides.

  3. 03

    Authentic
    Breakfast

    Wake up hungry. Leave happy. From classic eggs your way and hearty breakfast meats to fluffy pancakes, French toast, crepes, chilaquiles, breakfast burritos, Monte Cristo, cinnamon rolls, and fresh granola yogurt — we’ve got something for every morning craving.

    Choose from 5+ signature omelets or build your own, and don’t miss our Blue Corn Pancakes, a unique Moab favorite that’s not something you find everywhere. Complete your morning with fresh coffee, hot cocoa, tea, juices, and more.

    Big breakfast. Big flavors. A true Moab morning starts here.

  4. 04

    Moab Grill
    Desserts

    Saving room for something sweet? Indulge in our Classic Cheesecake, Mexican Chocolate Cake, Pie du Jour, Vanilla Crème Brûlée, and Ice Cream Sundae — the perfect way to finish your meal.

    From rich and chocolatey to creamy and refreshing, there’s a sweet ending for every craving.
